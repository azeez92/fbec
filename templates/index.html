About the Commission

The Federal Banditry Eradication Commission (FBEC) is dedicated to supporting national efforts to combat banditry through strategic coordination, public engagement, and collaboration with relevant security stakeholders.

Promoting Peace and Public Safety

FBEC works to strengthen community participation, encourage timely reporting of security threats, and support initiatives that enhance peace, safety, and stability across Nigeria.

Through partnerships with government institutions, security agencies, traditional leaders, and local communities, the Commission promotes intelligence sharing, public awareness, and coordinated responses to security challenges.

Our Operations

The Federal Banditry Eradication Commission (FBEC) coordinates strategic initiatives that strengthen national security, enhance public safety, and foster collaboration with communities and relevant stakeholders.

Public Advisory & Reporting Guidelines

Please follow these guidelines when reporting security-related incidents. Your cooperation helps ensure that reports are processed efficiently.

What should I report?

Report suspicious activities, abandoned camps, kidnappings, armed attacks, or any information that may assist relevant security authorities.

Provide Accurate Information

Include the location, date, time, and a clear description of the incident. Accurate information helps improve response and coordination.

Protect Your Safety

Do not confront or follow suspected criminals. Move to a safe location and report the incident through the appropriate channels.

Attach Supporting Evidence

Where possible, provide photographs, videos, or other relevant information that may assist authorities in verifying your report.

Can I remain anonymous?

Yes. Anonymous reports may be submitted. However, providing your contact information may assist authorities if further clarification is required.

What happens after I submit a report?

Reports are reviewed and, where appropriate, forwarded to the relevant authorities for assessment and any necessary action.

Avoid False Information

Submit only truthful and verifiable information. Deliberately false reports may hinder legitimate security efforts and waste valuable resources.

Emergency Situations

If there is an immediate threat to life or property, contact the appropriate emergency or security services without delay before submitting an online report.
